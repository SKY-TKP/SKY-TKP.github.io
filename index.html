System Design

MATCHROOM · AI Influencer Director

How brand rules become a published week of creator posts, on the same architecture as the VIBES LAB Music Director system.

Highlights

One Ranking Engine

A single engine powers every creator choice: weekly AI drafts, recommendations and the shortlist picker. It filters by brand rules, scores 7 weighted factors (content fit, tone, engagement rate, brand signature, history, language, trend), then re-ranks for agency caps and Thai-content share.

Approval Workflow & Rotation

Weekly programs move from AI draft to Director review to publish, and any edit to a published slot goes back for re-approval. Shortlists rotate one creator per slot per day, with no repeat in the same slot within 3 days.

AI Guardrails & Testing

AI can only pick creators from the roster, misquoted numbers are replaced and logged, and brands see plain-language reasons instead of scores. Built in vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript, covered by 140 automated click-through checks in headless Chrome.

Built on the Music Director Model

Every piece maps one to one: shops become brands, the song library becomes a creator roster, playlists become shortlists and royalties become creator fees. The engine keeps the same weights, and AI drafts and explains but never decides.

Deep Dive

How Many Primogems Are Enough?

Exact probability laws and risk-optimal budgets for CxRy targets under pity and Capturing Radiance. Technical monograph, version 1.0, 27 October 2026.

Highlights

Data from 1.2 Billion Rolls

Pulled per-banner pity counts from the Paimon.moe public API: 68 character and 67 weapon banners (Dec 2022 – Oct 2026). Profile-likelihood MLE recovers soft pity at rolls 74 / 63 with slopes 0.060 / 0.070, and a loss-pool estimator puts the post-5.0 50/50 win rate at 58.75% ± 1.03%.

An Exact Probability Engine

Hazard → single-5-star PMF; a 4×4 probability generating matrix M(z) for the Capturing Radiance counter; a shifted binomial for the one-Fate-Point weapon banner; n-fold convolutions for the joint and total roll law. Every PMF sums to 1 within 10−12, and C6R5 runs in about 50 ms.

Risk-Optimal Budgets

Chance-constrained budgets, CVaR and expected shortfall, priced in US dollars. Case study C2R1 with 64,000 Primogems: expected need 53,715, success 80.25% (89.48% with Starglitter), 90%-safe budget 69,920, expected top-up $17.04.

Validated by Simulation

A separate roll-by-roll simulator that shares no formulas with the engine (106 runs) matches every exact result inside the 95% DKW band: P(R ≤ 400) is 80.25% exact against 80.18% simulated.

01 · Data

  • Official disclosuresBase rates 0.6% / 0.7%, hard pity 90 / 80, 50/50 and 75/25, Radiance 0.018%

  • Risk measuresMean, SD, quantiles, CVaR, expected shortfall

The Capturing Radiance chain

Since Version 5.0 a lost 50/50 can be rescued by Capturing Radiance, driven by a hidden counter s of consecutive losses. Each featured copy costs one 5-star (a win or a Radiance) or two (a loss, then the guarantee). Writing that cost as a power of z turns the counter into a 4×4 matrix of polynomials, and multiplying the matrices adds up the cost along every path.

Data Model

BOM Calculator · From Supplier Price Sheets to Product Cost

How messy supplier sheets become one clean price list, how the data is modeled for PostgreSQL, and what a costing query returns. All values are dummy data.

Highlights

1 · Prepare

Each sheet is pulled in as-is (60 columns). Columns are found by header name, not position, so purchasing can keep adding dated price columns, and the latest dated price wins. A sync guard stops the run if any sheet suddenly loses more than 20% of its rows.

2 · Transform

Every row becomes one schema: purchase unit, pack, MOQ, lot, price tier and currency converted to THB; liquids convert to grams by density. Cross-checks flag a blank currency from a foreign supplier, one name priced 3× apart, or a pack that disagrees with the per-kg column.

3 · SQL Logic

Set-based steps: an append-only registry issues SKUs that never renumber, checked overrides beat source values, and the costing query turns a formula into an order: grams per piece → need with loss → round up to MOQ and pack → THB per piece.

4 · Database

Every submit writes a new BOM version plus a price snapshot of each line, so old versions never change when prices do. Status, the active version (latest approved) and the dashboard all come from two tables: versions and lines.
